How P2P Mesh & Persistent Storage Work

1. Persistent IndexedDB: All uploaded files and received mesh transfers are stored directly inside your browser's persistent database. Closing or restarting the browser will NOT delete your files.

2. Device Password Lock: Your account username and encrypted password lock access on this device. You can lock your session using the "Lock" button in the top bar.

3. Automatic Mesh Synchronization: When other peers open the website, online nodes automatically exchange missing file slots and updates seamlessly!
